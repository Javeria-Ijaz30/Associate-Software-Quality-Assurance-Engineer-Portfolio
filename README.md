I am a Software QA Engineer based in Lahore, Pakistan, with a BS in Computer Science from UET Lahore. I test JoClaims, an insurance claims management platform (web and mobile), and I also prepare requirements documents (SRS and BRS) for development teams.

- **Manual testing:** functional and regression testing, test case design, defect reporting and retesting
- **API testing:** request and response validation with Postman
- **Integration testing:** end-to-end workflows between connected systems
- **Requirements:** gathering and clarifying business requirements, SRS and BRS documents
- **Ways of working:** Agile and Scrum, Jira, ClickUp, Trello
